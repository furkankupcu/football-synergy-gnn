ⓘ How are the score and labels computed?
• Final Score = role pct × 0.80 + rating pct × 0.20, weighted by log(matches). In other words: league percentile on the role metrics + match experience.
• If a Style Target is active, the score is re-weighted by the fit with that style.
• Team Tier: from the selected season's standings; bottom half = Available (easier to sign), top half = Locked (hard).
• Status: the player's current contract/injury status (from the data source).

🧪 Tactical Lab — How to Use
  1. Left panel: type your own team into the "anchor team" box → the squad locks to it. Switch to 🌍 transfer mode to get suggestions from all leagues.
  2. Centre: pick a formation. Drag a player from the list onto the pitch, or click an empty slot and pick from the GNN suggestions.
  3. Right panel: model synergy score + suggestion details. optimize_lineup() lets the GNN pick the best XI.
  4. Purple badge = transfer player (not in the anchor team). Green = from the current squad.
